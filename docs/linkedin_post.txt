🔴 Brake fade has caused accidents at Le Mans, the Nürburgring, and on public roads. This ML system warns you before it happens.

POST:
Brake fade occurs when brake components overheat and friction coefficient drops — often suddenly and dangerously. It's a known risk in motorsport, heavy trucks on mountain descents, and even high-performance road cars.

In this project, I built a 3-class early warning classifier (No Fade / Mild / Severe) using thermal sensor + pedal signal data.

Features: Rotor temperature (front/rear), caliper temperature, pad wear, pedal force, deceleration, consecutive stop count, approach speed.

Results:
✅ CV Accuracy = 89.7%
✅ CV F1-macro = 88.6%
✅ AUC = 0.97+ for severe fade class (most critical)
✅ Prediction intervals generated via probability calibration

Key finding: Rotor temperature and consecutive stops are the two dominant predictors — consistent with brake thermal physics.

Application: Real-time CAN-bus integration → driver warning → autonomous brake force modulation (AEB adaptation).
